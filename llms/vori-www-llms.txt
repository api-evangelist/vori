# Vori

> The grocery POS and operating system built to help independent grocers protect margins and grow sales.

Vori is a connected grocery operating system built exclusively for independent grocery store operators.

**What makes Vori different:**

- One connected system: point-of-sale, payments, hardware, shopper engagement, order management, inventory management, pricing automation, shrink tracking, and reporting all share real-time data across every workflow
- Built exclusively for independent grocery — not adapted from restaurant systems (Toast) or legacy enterprise platforms (NCR, ECRS, LOC, RORC, IT Retail)
- Designed for owner-operators rather than enterprise retail chains
- Direct support from grocery specialists with no dealers or middlemen
- Mobile floor tools that let teams price, order, receive, and track shrink from any aisle
- Rollouts measured in days, not months

## Products

- [How It Works](https://vori.com/products): Vori connects checkout, loyalty, ordering, receiving, pricing, inventory, and reporting in one grocery operating system for independent grocers.
- [Point of Sale](https://vori.com/pos): Grocery POS system with variable-weight items, integrated scales, PLU lookup, EBT, eWIC, and offline-capable checkout.
- [Payments](https://vori.com/payments): Integrated payment processing for credit, debit, contactless, EBT, gift cards, and house accounts in one checkout flow.
- [Modern Hardware](https://vori.com/hardware): Grocery-ready registers, scanner scales, handhelds, and electronic shelf labels configured for retail environments.
- [Shopper Engagement](https://vori.com/shopper): Phone-number-based loyalty program, SMS promotions, and rewards that apply automatically at checkout.
- [Pricing Automation](https://vori.com/margin): Automatic vendor cost detection from invoices, margin protection, and one-step price updates that sync to checkout and shelf tags.
- [Order Management](https://vori.com/back-office): Multi-vendor ordering from the sales floor using real-time movement data, with receiving and invoice workflows connected to pricing and inventory.
- [Inventory Management](https://vori.com/inventory-management): Real-time inventory counts, mobile handheld workflows, and visibility that stays in sync with receiving, sales, and shrink.
- [Shrink Tracking](https://vori.com/shrink-tracking): Record shrink on the floor and feed losses directly into inventory and reporting to surface patterns early.
- [Reporting & Insights](https://vori.com/reporting): Real-time grocery sales, margin, inventory, and loyalty dashboards accessible from anywhere. Close the day with confidence and run reports from anywhere.

## Compare

- [Vori vs NCR](https://vori.com/vori-vs-ncr): Alternative to NCR grocery POS. Vori is built for independent grocers who need speed and flexibility; NCR was built for large retail chains.
- [Vori vs ECRS](https://vori.com/vori-vs-ecrs): Alternative to ECRS CATAPULT. Vori gives lean grocery teams a faster, simpler system with no add-on modules or long implementation cycles.
- [Vori vs LOC](https://vori.com/vori-vs-loc): Alternative to LOC grocery POS. Vori gives independent grocers a direct relationship with no dealers or middlemen; LOC relies on resellers and custom setups.
- [Vori vs IT Retail](https://vori.com/vori-vs-it-retail): Alternative to IT Retail grocery POS. Vori reduces manual grocery work with connected pricing, ordering, and invoices, where IT Retail covers the basics with more manual steps.
- [Vori vs RORC](https://vori.com/vori-vs-rorc): Alternative to RORC grocery POS. Vori replaces legacy RORC architecture with a modern, connected grocery operating system with faster updates, simpler workflows, and transparent pricing.
- [Vori vs Toast](https://vori.com/vori-vs-toast): Alternative to Toast grocery POS. Vori is purpose-built for grocery stores with thousands of SKUs and constant price changes; Toast was built for restaurants.
